## Analyze: Nutcracker
Product patent: https://patents.google.com/patent/USD452637S1/en
Description: A nutcracker is a tool used that when a tough seed such as a walnut is placed correctly between the two central applicators of force (in this case of the patent, the two cylinders) and when the handle is gripped and pulled the two moving pieced will transfer the force of the effort of you pulling on the handle to the applicators of force, the two cylinders, and move them to the walnut and use that force applied to easily break the tough shell.

While the patent displays a handle and two crushing pieces that transfer the force between the handle to the moving pieces, the more modern applicator is simply a plier-like device with two sturdy handles you grip and squeeze tightly to crush the walnut in between the tough pieces away from your hand. Both models are actually quite practical to use, which makes a tough job like breaking a walnut much easier and less time consuming. This makes this model in the patent file valid for its application/use.
