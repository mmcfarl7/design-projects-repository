## Analyze: Nutcracker
Product patent: https://patents.google.com/patent/USD452637S1/en
Description: A nutcracker is a tool used that when a tough seed such as a walnut is placed correctly between the two central applicators of force (in this case of the patent, the two cylinders) and when the handle is gripped and pulled the two moving pieced will transfer the force of the effort of you pulling on the handle to the applicators of force, the two cylinders, and move them to the walnut and use that force applied to easily break the tough shell.

While the patent displays a handle and two crushing pieces that transfer the force between the handle to the moving pieces, the more modern applicator is simply a plier-like device with two sturdy handles you grip and squeeze tightly to crush the walnut in between the tough pieces away from your hand. Both models are actually quite practical to use, which makes a tough job like breaking a walnut much easier and less time consuming. This makes this model in the patent file valid for its application/use.

The authors of the patent of the nutcracker are Arthur David ReedClarence Arthur Reed. If you take notice of the various figures 1 through 10 in the patent website, you'll notice in angled figures such as figures 1 and 2 on page two that the nutcracker design has a "cap" or curved stopper to help hold the walnut in place so it doesn't slip out and a possible little spike or bump inside that cap so during the breaking process the crack doesn't end up uneven. Some other possible products you can choose that are as equally simple are a pair of pliers, which are similar to some modern designs and do serve the same purpose of applying force from the handle that you squeeze. Another less practical but equally simple device you could use is a hammer, which you grip firmly and swing, transferring your applied force and increase it with the aid of gravity and increased mass from the hammer head, transferring your applied force somewhere else, in this case that is targeted at a walnut.
## Decide
Homepage Identity: The homepage is one of the most important parts of a website or a portfolio. If a website is messy or sloppy or generic, a viewer's retention will quickly be lost. It is a common science that many viewers of the ancient newspaper would see the big letters and image of the headline on the front page and would make their assumptions on the rest of the paper *BEFORE* reading the smaller and less noticeable text surrounding the headline. The homepage is important because if made with enough practicality and creativity then a reader or viewer of the homepage can immediately tell what the page is about, where things can be located, and what they expect to find exploring the rest of the website or portfolio.
