## Decide

Ultimately the machinery handbook is what led me through this assignment along with a mild bit of help from the lecture and walkthrough videos. This assignment required many calculations and in the end it required firm decisions about certain materials from the McMaster's website. I chose the 3/8th's diameter Polyurethane rubber 20.5 inches circumference round belt since it followed through with my calculations and I also chose the journal bearing material to be porous bronze as per table 4 in the machinery handbook.

## Communicate

The engineering lesson I learned from this assignment is how to break down pulley calculations into simple easy steps and get all the data one would need from a few given parameters. This is probably an oversimplified assignment of a much more rigorous and complicated setup, but we all have to start out at level 0 before we reach level 1, and I think I did fairly well on this assignment going from steps 1 through 5 and finishing off this assignment with confidence.

Total time: 1.5 to 2 hours.
